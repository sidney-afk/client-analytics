# Speed map, re-measured: every tab, warm load and switching

**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Session:** Keel
**Compares against:** `docs/audits/2026-09-23-speed-map.md` (the "map of the 23rd").
**Measurement only: no app code changed, reads only, test client only.**

---

## 1. Headline

- **The three slow switches are gone.** Switching from the calendar to Workload went
  from 4,284 ms to **43 ms**, SyncLinear from 3,996 ms to **128 ms** (median of 8),
  TikTok upload from 3,527 ms to **27 ms**. Every one of the nine switchable tabs now
  switches in well under a quarter of a second.
- **The slow warm loads collapsed onto one shared floor.** Workload 6,008 → **1,147 ms**,
  SyncLinear 2,822 → **1,150 ms**, TikTok upload 2,728 → **1,066 ms**, Kasper review
  2,401 → **1,247 ms**. On this rig, nine of fifteen tabs now finish between 1,049 and
  1,337 ms, and for all of them the last thing to arrive is the same shared sign-in check
  (`key-verify`, ~1.0 s), not their own data.
- **Still slow:** Analytics (3,339 ms, wide spread), SMM reports (2,326 ms), Calendar
  (1,793 ms).
- **Still broken:** reloading either Kasper subtab. Hiring **0 of 5** reloads rendered
  (unchanged from the 23rd); onboarding **0 of 5** (was 2 of 6). A fresh open of the
  hiring link does render (about 1.6 s), so this is the reload path only, as §7 of the
  23rd described.
- **SyncLinear has no real bottleneck left of its own.** It now paints from its saved
  copy; the deliverables read is no longer on its critical path at all. What it waits on
  is the shared entry gate every tab waits on (§5).

## 2. Rig, and how far it matches the 23rd

Same driver shape: Playwright Chromium, 1440×950, the live site, a persistent profile
signed in as an admin staff member, readiness polled on `requestAnimationFrame`, warm
= `page.reload()` of a primed page, and **every warm load cross-checked** by the
`index.html` transfer size (300 bytes = a revalidated cache hit). All counted loads
passed that check; one cold load that slipped into the Samples set was discarded.
Same readiness selectors as §3 of the 23rd, with one change noted in §3.

**Not the same machine.** The 23rd ran on a profile the owner signed into by hand; that
profile was deleted. This run is a cloud sandbox whose traffic passes through an egress
proxy, signed in with the staff role key through the real `key-verify` function. That
adds latency to every request. The effect is visible on tabs that did not change:

| unchanged fast tabs | 23rd | today |
|---|---|---|
| Templates | 587 | 1,069 |
| Submit | 608 | 1,049 |
| TikTok pilot | 590 | 1,072 |

So **this rig's floor is ~1,050 ms, about 450 ms above the 23rd's.** Read every
number below with that in mind: a tab that reads ~1,100 ms today is at the floor, and
the real gains on the formerly slow tabs are *larger* than the raw differences suggest.
The next map should be taken on this same sandbox rig so the two compare directly.

5 warm reloads per tab (after one priming load), except Samples at 4 (one cold load discarded, see §3); medians of an even count are the mean of the middle two, 3 switches per tab (8 for SyncLinear).

## 5. What each tab waits on now

At the instant each tab's content appeared, every request that had already finished was
recorded (median warm load). "Entry gate" below means the requests every tab makes
before its own data: the page's scripts, `key-verify`, and the runtime-flag reads.

| tab | requests before content | last to finish before content |
|---|---|---|
| Submit, Templates, TikTok pilot, TikTok upload, Kasper review, Filming Plans, SyncLinear | **19–20** | entry gate only: `key-verify` @933–1,056 · `syncview_runtime_flags` · supabase-js from the CDN |
| Workload, Time off | 21 | entry gate, plus `workload_issues` @563 on Workload |
| Samples | 23 | `sample_reviews` @1,587 · entry gate |
| SMM reports | 17 | **`smm-weekly-reports` twice** @2,167 and @2,312 · `templates` @1,996 |
| Calendar | 33 | `calendar_posts` · a Google Sheets read · n8n `templates-get` · `caption_prompts` |
| Analytics | 29 | **four Google Sheets reads**, the last at 5.9–9.1 s |
| Kasper hiring / onboarding (reload) | 39 | never ready; `syncview_runtime_flags` read **8 times** and the sheet queue 8 times before the 10 s window closed |

What changed, and what did not:

1. **Data a tab does not show is no longer on the critical path of Workload, Kasper
   review or TikTok upload.** On the 23rd each waited on Google Sheets (TopVideos,
   Metrics, Competitor Briefs); today none of them do.
2. **SyncLinear's "three fetches" are off the critical path.** They were one paged
   read (the server returns at most 1,000 rows at a time), and the tab now paints from
   its saved copy before that read finishes. Not one deliverables request is among what
   SyncLinear waits on.
3. **The entry gate is now the whole cost for most tabs.** For seven tabs the very last
   request before content is `key-verify` or a runtime-flag read. The flag table is read
   **4 to 8 times per load** (it was 2 on the 23rd). Collapsing those into one read is
   now the single change that would move the most tabs. `key-verify` itself **is** the
   security gate (`100-onboarding-staff-controls.js.part`, "Staff entry gate": shell
   admission and data both wait on it and fail closed); only the saved identity is a
   paint hint. So staff or client data must keep waiting on it. What can move is
   everything around it: starting it earlier or in parallel with the flag read, and
   painting only non-sensitive chrome before it answers. The 23rd's suggestion to render
   tabs before it resolves was wrong on this point and should not be followed.
4. **Bytes are not reported for warm loads.** With a warm cache almost everything is
   served locally, so transfer sizes read near zero; the 23rd's 172 KB figure was a cold
   figure and is not comparable. Counts are reported instead.

## 6. What is still slow, in order

1. **Kasper subtab reloads, broken, not slow.** Hiring 0/5, onboarding 0/5. Unchanged
   diagnosis from §7 of the 23rd (the capability gate overriding the subtab after it
   resolves); the repeated flag reads while stuck point the same way.
2. **Analytics, 3,339 ms and erratic (2.0 to 9.3 s).** Waits on four Google Sheets
   reads. Its spread is set by Google's response time, not by the app.
3. **SMM reports, 2,326 ms, slower than the 23rd (993 ms).** It reads
   `smm-weekly-reports` twice before showing anything, and the second read ends last.
   Worth checking whether the first answer could paint.
4. **Calendar, 1,793 ms, ~700 ms above the floor.** Waits on its posts plus a Google
   Sheets read and two template reads.
5. **The shared entry gate, ~1.0 s on this rig**, under every other tab.

## 7. Limits

- **Different machine from the 23rd** (§2). Compare today's numbers with each other
  and with the next sandbox run, not with the 23rd's absolute milliseconds.
- Warm only, as asked. Cold was not re-measured; the 23rd showed it does not reproduce.
- 5 loads and 3 switches per tab: enough to tell 4,284 ms from 43 ms, not for ±10% claims.
- One sandbox, one connection, one admin staff account, one test client with an empty
  samples queue and a small calendar.
- Interactions (§6 of the 23rd) were not re-measured.

## 8. Reproduction

The driver ran from a session scratchpad and is not committed, for the same reason as
the 23rd: it handles a staff credential. The browser reaches the live site through the
sandbox proxy by trusting only that proxy's certificate
(`--ignore-certificate-errors-spki-list` pinned to the proxy CA), and signs in by
posting the role key to `key-verify` and saving the returned identity, so no sign-in
screen is involved. The method in §2 and the selectors in §3 of both maps are enough to
rebuild it. **Trap:** never open the app with `?sxr=0` during a run; it is the sticky
per-browser switch that turns Samples off, and it silently makes Samples "never ready".
