7 · A carousel opens the card (client link, Month and Week)

Why it does nothing today: on the client link every Month/Week thumbnail gets svFrameThumbAttrs. A post whose video is not a Frame.io link (a carousel) gets data-fv-none, and with the viewer switched on for the client svFrameThumbOpen swallows the click on purpose ("a post with no video opens nothing"). Reels have a Frame link, so they open the card.
