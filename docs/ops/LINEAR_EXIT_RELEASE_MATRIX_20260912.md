## Order of decisions

1. Finish code, full recovery coverage, integration and the affected isolated
   acceptance checks. Publish a reviewed exact revision and one evidence index.
2. Have the next session review that revision and the unresolved gates. A quick
   review is not an instruction to skip a listed build gap.
3. Only under separate owner authorization, capture hosted pre-state and durable
   recovery material, validate target compatibility and perform installation in
   the reviewed order. Preserve current authority and all dormant settings until
   their own acceptance conditions pass.
4. Activate native capabilities only with their required hosted observations.
   Retain operational Linear dependencies until the separate retirement gate is
   satisfied. Do not turn an installation receipt into a retirement receipt.
5. Retire Linear only after the admission/drain protocol and canonical B5 gate
   are resolved and proved. Retention, cancellation and irreversible deletion
   remain separate decisions.

## Dormant state and recovery

The existing refusing retirement activation routine stays refusing. Do not
change authority, cutoff, sender, gateway-wake or reconciler-apply settings as
part of publishing the build. The prepared sign-off workflow stays manual and
dry-run by default. No n8n execution or edit belongs to this preparation.

For a failed installation, stop at the failed step and preserve its receipt.
Resume only after classifying the actual installed prefix and reviewing its
transaction boundaries. Do not replay an obsolete replacement migration or
restore stale business data over accepted new saves. A full-data restoration
requires the reviewed admission boundary and an explicit disposition for every
accepted mutation after its snapshot.

This matrix records outstanding requirements; it does not waive them. In
particular, typed native outbox completions do not silently redefine B5's literal
zero-new-row condition as zero provider debt.
