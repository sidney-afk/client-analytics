Archived cards: see and restore

Static mock-up for owner review. Calendar first, then Samples. Every screen below is also saved as a picture in screens/.

For the reviewer

This page shows the whole sequence for Calendar and for Samples, using the app's own styles. Nothing here is wired to anything; names are made up. Plan: docs/plans/2026-09-29-calendar-unarchive.md.

Open question 1, needs the owner's answer before the build. The decision says each work item goes back to the exact status it had. For an item whose status before the archive was SMM approval or Tweaks, the database announces any staff move into those two statuses to the client's channel (read from migrations/2026-09-09-native-notification-outbox.sql, the trigger production_notification_status_intent_after). So restoring such an item would send the client a second "ready for SMM approval" or "needs tweaks" message. The build instruction says to stop and report if a restore sends a client notification, so I am asking now instead. Options: (a) accept the message; (b) put those items back but silence the message, which needs a change to production-write and an owner deploy; (c) leave those two kinds of item in Backlog and say "left in Backlog so the client is not told again" in the dialog. My recommendation is (c). Every other status (To do, In progress, Client approval, Done) is not announced by that trigger. In the 2026-09-28 park run, 14 of 46 parked items came from SMM approval.
  1. Wording of the work item lines follows the decision: "back to SMM approval", "to To do", "stays Done", "left alone, it moved since".
  2. Samples call the second item a Thumbnail and have no dates, so the Samples screens drop the date line.
  3. Assumption A1: an item that is in Backlog with no readable record of where it came from is left alone, with the words "left alone, no record of where it was". The decision did not cover this case.
  4. Dashed purple outline on the menu item marks the new item for the review only. It will not ship.
