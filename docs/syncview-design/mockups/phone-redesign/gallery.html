SyncView on a phone, redesigned

These are pictures of the real SyncView page, built from the code and run on a phone-sized screen with made-up posts and notes. "Today" is the page as it is now. "Proposed" is the same page with the new design laid over it. Nothing is built yet. Tap any picture to see it full size.

Decisions for you

1. Sheet shows a list first, with the grid one tap awayThe list shows each post's status and new notes. The grid keeps the Instagram look. I recommend: yes.
2. A bar at the bottom with Sheet, Calendar and ReviewIt replaces the switch at the top and the big purple "Review posts" bar, and your thumb can reach it. I recommend: yes.
3. Delete moves off each noteReply, Mark done and No reply needed stay on every note. Delete moves to a long press, so nobody deletes by accident. I recommend: yes.
4. Conversations as a plain list, not chat bubblesSeveral people talk in one conversation (client, team, Kasper), so a name and a line read faster than coloured bubbles. I recommend: the list.
5. On the client's page, Approve and Ask for a change stay at the bottom of the screenThey are always under the client's thumb, whatever they are looking at. I recommend: yes.
6. Square corners everywhereButtons, badges and chips get 4 px corners, cards and panels 6 px. No pills or bubbles. I recommend: yes.
Phone width in pixels: small Android, iPhone 14, iPhone Pro Max

Calendar, Sheet tab

What is wrong today

  • The first post starts 272 px down, a third of the screen. Above it: a big title, Tabs, a menu, the client name, a three-way switch, "9 posts" and a grid/list switch.
  • Every tile repeats its title, status and full date in white text over the picture. Only 6 posts fit on screen.
  • No tile shows notes (0 of 9), so you cannot tell which post has a new change or comment.
  • The list view is one post per 1,421 px card, so you see one post per screen.
  • The purple "Review posts" bar at the bottom repeats the Review tab.

What changes

  • The client name is the title. New post, Messages (with its count) and the menu are icons at the top right.
  • One line per post: picture, title, a coloured square with the status in words, and the date. Badges on the right show new notes.
  • Sheet, Calendar and Review sit in a bottom bar, with the Review count on it.

B · STAFF

Notes on a post

What is wrong today

  • Each note has its own box of Reply, Mark done and Delete: 10 boxes and 10 Delete buttons for 10 notes. Delete is as easy to hit as Reply.
  • 245 words on one screen. Every note carries a coloured circle, labels like "TWEAK #1" and "Team only", and the part in capitals.
  • The message box takes 173 px: "About" with three part buttons, a lock button, a two-part Send button and a help line.
  • Nothing says what still needs you. Open changes and unanswered questions sit between old, settled notes.

What changes

  • "Needs you" at the top: open changes and client questions nobody has answered yet. Everything else is under "Earlier".
  • One small icon says what each note is: orange pencil for a change, blue bubble for a comment, blue arrow for "waiting for an answer", violet eye for Kasper. Filled means new to you.
  • Reply, Mark done and No reply needed are plain words under the note, with no box. Long threads fold to "3 replies".
  • Tabs at the top filter by part (All, Video, Caption) and hide parts with no notes.
  • The message box is one row of three choices (part, who sees it, comment or change) and one line to type in.

C · STAFF

Messages

What is wrong today

  • A conversation has two headers, one under the other: "Messages, Close, Tabs, •••", then "‹, title, •••".
  • Every message has up to two lines of labels before its text: name, role, part in capitals, CHANGE REQUEST, To client, time and Reply. That is 7 capital labels and 12 pill shapes on one screen, and only 5 messages fit.
  • Each message sits in its own rounded box.
  • Mark unread and Open card are hidden behind "•••".
  • The list shows initials ("SB") instead of the post's picture. There is no "To answer" filter.

What changes

  • The list: New, To answer and All. Each row has the post's picture, title, client, the last line and its badge.
  • A conversation has one header with Open card in words. Names show once per run of messages, and there are no boxes.
  • A client's change request shows as one orange line that opens the post, because changes are handled on the post (Beacon's rule D2).
  • A question waiting for an answer has Reply and No reply needed right under it. Team-only messages show a small lock.

D · CLIENT LINK

The client's review page

What is wrong today

  • 35 round or pill shapes on the first screen: the tabs, the three steps, the buttons and the post chips.
  • Approve starts 663 px down, under the caption, so the client scrolls to answer.
  • The caption sits in two boxes, one inside the other. A "130 characters" line and a "Content calendar" subtitle add text the client does not need.
  • A greyed-out "Finish reviewing 2 of 3" button is always on screen.
  • The client's own messages are lilac boxes, each with a "Delete note" button.

What changes

  • Ask for a change and Approve are in a bar fixed at the bottom of the screen. Finish reviewing joins the bar once every part has an answer.
  • Tabs and the three steps are flat text with a line under the current one. The caption sits straight on the page.
  • The conversation loses its boxes and Delete buttons (Delete moves to a long press).

The client's page on the Caption step.

BUILD ORDER

Small PRs, each behind a switch

Each step is switched on for your two staff ids and the test client first, the same way as the calm Workload calendar. Saving works exactly as it does today. Only the look and the number of taps change.

  1. Shared phone look. Corners, lines, the status square and the badge (icon and number), in one place. Nothing visible changes.
  2. Client review page. The bottom bar, flat tabs and steps, and a quieter conversation. Styles only, on the page that already exists.
  3. Sheet list and the bottom bar. Grid kept as the second view.
  4. A post opened from the Sheet. Notes near the top, statuses as rows.
  5. Notes. The look first. "Needs you" and No reply needed come with Beacon's Notes step.
  6. Messages. The list and the conversation. "To answer" comes with Beacon's Messages step.
  7. Badges on the Sheet tiles and rows. Comes with Beacon's step 3, when the server gives counts per kind.
  8. Clean-up. Remove the old phone styles once every step is on for everyone.

How these were made: docs/syncview-design/mockups/phone-redesign/. The offline test harness runs the real page with made-up data and answers every request locally. preview.js draws the proposed design from the page's own data, and the app never loads it.
