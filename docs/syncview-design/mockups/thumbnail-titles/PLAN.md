# Thumbnail titles from the filming plan: plan

Session Titlecard, written 2026-10-23 20:24 UTC (`date -u`). **Approved by the owner 2026-10-23 (all of section 10) and built in the follow-up PR (OPEN_REPAIRS 476);** the measured cost is in that entry. One change from the plan while building: the skip message says "No video N in the October plan" only when the tab's list stops before N, and "Video N not matched in the October plan" otherwise.

Mock-ups in this folder, pictures in `screens/`. The mock-ups themselves ship nothing.

Owner's ask (2026-10-23): on a thumbnail sub-issue, a button next to **Edit** on the description
that goes to the filming plan, finds the right batch, finds this video and writes its thumbnail title
with the existing thumbnail title prompt. More important: one button on the parent card, on the right
of the **Sub-issues N** header, that does it for every thumbnail sub-issue at once, with a building
indicator on each row and a toast that stays until everything is finished.

## 0. What already exists (measured, so the plan builds on it instead of beside it)

Checked against `main` (9098505) and the live database, read only, 2026-10-23 20:10 UTC. Counts only.

- **An automatic writer is already live.** `supabase/functions/thumbnail-titles` (OPEN_REPAIRS 391,

  empty calendar-made thumbnail, reads the client's filming plan (`planTabs` in

  matches the video by the card's "Video N" (or by name, through the model), asks Claude once per
  tab, checks the title is short and in the plan's own words, and writes
  `Thumbnail title (generated by AI): …` into the **description**, only while it is still empty.
  Where it cannot, it writes one line starting `Needs info: …`.
- **It runs once, at creation, and mostly cannot help yet.** Last 7 days: 79 items handled, **18
  got a title, 61 got "Needs info"** (24 plan empty, 20 no tab for the month, 17 not matched). The
  usual cause is timing: the thumbnail is created before the month's plan tab is written. Nothing
  re-tries it later. Open thumbnails right now: 69 empty, 58 "Needs info", 34 AI titles, 252 with
  text a person wrote.
- **No button exists anywhere.** The Calendar's only control is the prompt editor ("…" menu,
  "Thumbnail title prompt"). The function is callable only by the timer (Vault key, no CORS).
- **A second, older writer** runs inside `production-write` on Submit-tab batches (first tab only,
  20k characters). Not touched by this plan.
- **The Filming plan row** on the parent card's Assets grid resolves batch link, then the post's
  batch, then the client's plan. Measured: in the last 60 days 379 of 388 batches carry a link and
  **all 379 point at the client's one plan Doc**. So "go to the filming plan" always means the
  client's Doc, and "find the batch" means **find the right tab inside it** (the house format is one
  Docs tab per month; some plans use one per week).
- **Batch sizes:** 333 calendar batches in 90 days, 133 with more than one thumbnail, largest 29.

**So the feature is: a manual, on-demand run of the writer that already exists, with a better tab
rule, that can also replace a stale "Needs info" line, and that shows its work.** No second prompt,
no second plan reader, no change to `production-write` (no Section 4 re-pin).

## 1. Choosing the batch (the plan tab)

The owner's rule: *the latest batch, or the previous one when the latest is the one still being
worked on right now.* Made exact:

1. Read the plan's tabs (`planTabs`). Keep tabs that actually list videos (`planHasVideos`: a
   template or a one-word placeholder is not a batch).
2. Give each tab a date: the month or week in its **name** ("October 2026", "Week of Sept 28"),
   else the month its text mentions most (`tabMonth`), else its position in the Doc.
3. **Anchor = the date this parent's batch was created in SyncView** (the footage was filmed before
   it was submitted). A tab dated **after** the anchor is the plan being written for the next shoot:
   that is "the one still being worked on right now", and it is skipped.
4. **Pick the latest remaining tab** (dated on or before the anchor's month/week).
5. **Check it fits:** every video number on the batch's thumbnails must exist in that tab's list
   (a batch with "Video 9" cannot come from a tab with six videos). If it does not fit, try the one
   before it, once. Still no fit: stop and ask (see "two matches" below).
6. The chosen tab's name is **always shown**: in the toast ("From the October plan") and on every
   row's result, with **Use another tab** in the toast, so a wrong pick is visible and one tap away
   from being corrected. This is the permissive rule (AGENTS.md): show the choice, never hide it.

In the common case this gives the same tab as today's `pickTab` (the month the batch was made); it
differs exactly where the owner's rule is better: when next month's tab already exists.

## 2. Matching a sub-issue to its video

In order, first hit wins:

1. **Number.** "Video 4" in the post's card name, else "Thumbnail 4" on the sub-issue when the batch
   has more than one thumbnail (existing `numberFromCardName`, `numberFromItemTitle`). The model is
   told the number; the plan's own numbering is ignored and entries are counted from the top of the
   tab's main list (existing system prompt rule).
2. **Title.** No number: the card name ("Video 4 — Morning routine" gives "Morning routine") is
   matched by topic to the one video that clearly names it.
3. **Link.** Not used. Plans do not carry per-video links to SyncView items, and a Frame.io or Drive
   link in a plan is not reliable enough to match on. (Listed so nobody re-asks.)

What happens when it does not work (nothing is ever guessed; nothing is written for these):

| Situation | Row shows | Description |
|---|---|---|
| No video N in the chosen tab, or no topic match | **Skipped: no video 9 in the October plan** | unchanged |
| Two videos could be it (repeated list, same topic twice) | **Skipped: two videos could match** | unchanged |
| Two tabs fit equally (two "October" tabs) | Toast asks **Which plan tab?** with the two names, before any title is made | unchanged |
| No filming plan linked to the client | Toast: **No filming plan linked. Add one in Clients.** | unchanged |
| Plan cannot be opened (not shared by link) | Toast: **The filming plan isn't shared by link, so it can't be read.** + how to fix | unchanged |
| Plan or tab is empty / a template | Toast: **The October plan has no videos yet.** | unchanged |
| Title not in the plan's own words (fails `grounded`) | **Skipped: no title could be grounded in the plan** | unchanged |

A manual run **never writes a "Needs info" line**: the reason is on screen, in the row and the toast,
for the person who asked. Only the background writer keeps writing those lines.

**Private Docs:** today's reader needs "anyone with the link can view". Recommended follow-up (not
in this feature): read with the service account (`pipeline-google` / `filming-plan-tabs` already hold
one) and fall back to the link reader, so a private plan works once it is shared with that account.

## 3. Where the title is written, and overwriting

**Recommended: the description**, in the label the team already knows:
`Thumbnail title (generated by AI, from the October plan): Morning routine that works`.

- Not the **title** field: "Thumbnail 4" is how the batch, the list and the matcher know which video
  it is. Renaming it breaks matching the next time and changes the list everybody scans.
- Not a **comment**: designers read the description; a comment is buried under the conversation and
  notifies people.

Overwrite rule, per row (decided on the server under a row lock, compare-and-set on the exact text
that was shown):

| Description now | What the run does |
|---|---|
| Empty | Writes the title. |
| A `Needs info:` line | Replaces it (machine text, nothing a person wrote). Row shows "was: Needs info". |
| A previous AI title | **Shows before → after, writes nothing until the person taps Use new.** |
| Anything a person wrote | **Shows before → after, writes nothing until the person taps Replace.** Default is keep. Replace keeps their text under the title (a "Previous description" line), never deletes it. |

**Undo** on every write for the life of the toast (restores the exact previous text, only if nobody
changed it since). Every change is in the issue's history like any other description edit.

## 4. Running many at once

One press = **one run** for the parent's thumbnail sub-issues.

- **One model call per plan tab**, not per title (the existing grouping). A batch of 12 costs about
  what one title costs. Cap: 30 thumbnails per run (largest batch measured: 29); more are done in a
  second call, same run.
- **Progress per row.** The run is stored server-side (the existing `thumbnail_title_queue` gains
  reason `manual`, a `run_id`, `requested_by`, `proposed_text`, `previous_brief`). The page watches
  the run (status call every 1.5 s while the toast is open). Stages: *Reading the filming plan* →
  *Finding the batch* → *Writing titles* (rows switch from waiting to building) → each row turns done,
  skipped, needs-you or failed as its answer is stored.
- **Retries.** A provider timeout, 429 or 5xx is retried twice inside the run with back-off (the
  function already has a 60 s per-call timeout and a JSON-only follow-up). Still failing: the row
  says **Failed — try again** with a Retry on that row (re-runs just that item).
- **Second click while running:** the button becomes **Writing titles… 3/8** and is not pressable;
  the server allows one active run per parent, so a second press from anywhere joins the same run.
- **Closing the tab mid-run:** the work is on the server, so it finishes. Titles that could be written
  are written; before → after proposals wait on the run. Reopening the card shows **2 suggested
  titles waiting** in the Sub-issues header and opens the same review. The per-minute timer finishes
  a run whose request died (lease expiry), as it does today.
- **Two people at once:** the second person sees **Being written by Sam…** with the same progress and
  can review the proposals too; the first Use new wins, the second gets "already changed".
- **A person edits a description while it runs:** the compare-and-set refuses the write; the row
  says **Changed while writing — kept yours**.

## 5. Who may press it

Today only **admin and SMM** keys may change a description; a creative (designer) key may never
(`staffOperationAllowed`, `production-write/policy.mjs`). Recommended: **the same rule**, so the
button sits beside Edit exactly where Edit works, checked on the server with the staff key
(`authorizeBrowserWrite`, as `thumbnail-title-prompts` does). For a designer the button is shown
greyed with the reason on its label ("Only SMMs and admins can write titles"), not hidden. Never on
client links. Shown on thumbnail sub-issues of every origin; a Samples item without a plan gets the
plain "no filming plan" answer rather than a hidden button (permissive rule). Owner decision 2 asks
whether designers should be allowed to press it.

## 6. Cost

Model: `claude-sonnet-5-5` (the writer's default), $2 per million tokens in, $10 out. The plan tab
dominates: a typical month tab is 2k to 10k tokens, the cap is 120k characters (about 30k tokens).

- **One title (sub-issue button):** one call, about **1 to 7 cents** (worst case about 10 cents with a
  maximum-size tab).
- **A whole batch (parent button):** the same single call per tab, about **2 to 10 cents per batch**,
  whatever its size. A borrowed-tab check adds one small call.
- **Today nothing records tokens.** Recommended: store `input_tokens` / `output_tokens` on each run so
  the real cost per batch is measured, not estimated; and a soft limit of 30 runs per person per hour.

## 7. What is logged

- **Run row** (`thumbnail_title_queue`, reason `manual`): who, when, parent, tab chosen, per-item
  outcome, tokens, `last_error` code. Never the title text or a plan line in function logs (existing
  rule: counts and codes only).
- **Issue history** (`deliverable_events`, `description_change`, surface `thumbnail_titles_manual`,
  actor = the person who pressed): one row per write, Replace or Undo.
- **Refused or failed writes** reported through `_writeUiRecordFailure`, like the other Production
  writes, so they reach the write-refusal log.

## 10. Decisions for the owner (each with a recommendation)

1. **Where the title goes.** Recommend the **description**, with the existing "Thumbnail title
   (generated by AI, from the October plan): …" label. Not the title field, not a comment.
2. **Who may press it.** Recommend **admins and SMMs only** (the people who can already edit a
   description). Alternative: let designers press it too, which needs a new exception in the write rules.
3. **Overwriting.** Recommend: empty and "Needs info" lines are replaced at once; an earlier AI title
   or anything a person wrote is **never replaced without a tap** (before → after, Keep mine is the
   default), and Replace keeps the old text under the title.
4. **The batch rule.** Recommend the rule in §1: the latest plan tab dated on or before the day the
   batch was created (a newer tab is the one still being written), checked against the video numbers,
   with the tab's name always shown and "Use another tab" one tap away.
5. **The background writer.** Recommend keeping it, but stop it writing "Needs info" lines into
   empty descriptions (61 of 79 last week), now that a person can press the button when the plan is
   ready. Alternative: keep the lines as they are.
6. **Private plans.** Recommend a follow-up to read plans with the service account the Docs-tab reader
   already has, so a plan that is not shared by link still works.
7. **Cost.** About 2 to 10 cents per batch, one AI call per plan tab. Recommend recording the real
   tokens per run from day one and a soft limit of 30 runs per person per hour.
