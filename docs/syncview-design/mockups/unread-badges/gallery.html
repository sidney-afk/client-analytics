How a card shows three or more kinds at once

Each new kind is one square segment with its icon and number, joined into one strip in a fixed order: change, comment, team, Kasper. What you have read but still need to do (a change not done, a comment to answer) follows as a second, outlined strip. A card shows at most four segments; anything beyond becomes "+1". No words on the card: hover the strip and it lists who and what, at once.

Recommendation: yes, one strip in a fixed order, at most four segments.

Already decided by you
  • The Messages number counts conversations with something new; an outlined number when only answers are owed. Change requests never count there.
  • A client's comment stays "Waiting for your answer" until someone replies or clicks "No reply needed".
  • No summary line above the calendar (removed).
  • Change requests stay on the card; the post's SMM and admins see "waiting for your answer"; one read for everything; reading on the phone clears the laptop within 30 seconds.

1. Card badges

Why it is obvious: the colour and icon say the kind, the number says how many; filled is new, outlined is waiting on you. Nothing else is written on the card.

2. Hover on a card

Why it is obvious: one line per note, starting with who wrote it and what it is; it opens instantly, no delay.

3. Notes on a card

Why it is obvious: "Needs you" sits under the title and each line jumps to its note; below, the conversation in time order, one hairline between notes, a thin colour line for the kind, and Reply, No reply needed or Mark done right under the note that needs it.

4. The Messages button

Why it is obvious: a square number on the corner that never moves the icon; hover says, in words, what it is counting.

5. The Messages inbox

Why it is obvious: three plain tabs (New, To answer, All) underlined like the rest of SyncView, one line of hairline per conversation, the post's name first, and the same badge strip as the cards.

6. A conversation

Why it is obvious: no bubbles, a flat log like a document: who, which part and when on one line, the words under it, a thin colour line for the kind. "Team only" is the one label kept, because it matters. A change request is one orange line pointing to the card.
