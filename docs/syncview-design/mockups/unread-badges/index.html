Unread badges, round 2

Two places, two jobs. The card's Notes is where change requests are worked: they stay on the card until they are Done. Messages is for conversation: comments, questions and replies that expect an answer. A filled badge means new for you. An outlined badge means it still needs you: orange for open changes, blue for something waiting for an answer.

3. Card badges, every kind and combination

Order on the button is always: changes (filled, then outlined), then comments (filled, then outlined), then staff and Kasper. At most three badges; a fourth folds into "+1".

4. Hover (long-press on a phone): what it is before you open it

Newest first, up to four lines: kind, who, which part, first words, when. Something waiting for an answer says so. Looking does not mark anything read.

5. Notes on a card: opening it does not lose what still needs you

Opening Notes clears the "new" fill, but a change stays open until Done and a comment stays Waiting for an answer until someone on the team replies or marks it "No reply needed". What still needs you is pinned at the top.

6. "I'll answer later": three ways it stays on your list

Automatic: a client comment or question stays "Waiting for an answer" after you read it, until anyone on the team replies or marks it "No reply needed". Mark unread: puts the fill back for you only. A Remind me action (later, optional) would bring it back at a chosen time.
