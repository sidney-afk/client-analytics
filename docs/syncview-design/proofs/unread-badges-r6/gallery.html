Part 2: the whole Messages page, same rule
  • Status words are quiet coloured words, never filled pills: "Waiting for your answer", "Change request", "Not done", "New". "Waiting for your answer" stands alone (no extra "New" next to it).
  • Notes: "TWEAK #1" reads "Change request"; "Team only" is the same small lock as in Messages.
  • Conversation header: no empty grey picture; "Mark unread" and "Open card" are plain words.
  • Day lines say Today, Yesterday, the weekday, or "Wed 14 Oct", as Slack does.
  • No "New" tag on each message. One thin red "New" line marks where the unread part starts, as Slack does.
  • A client's change request is one quiet line: "Casey asked for a change on the video · 12m ago · Open card".
  • Two messages in a row by the same person within five minutes show the picture and name once, as Slack does.
  • Reply caption uses the first name ("Replying to Casey").
