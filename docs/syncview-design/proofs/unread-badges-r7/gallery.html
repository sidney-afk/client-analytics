What changed
  • Seen but still needs you: on a card, just the coloured number, no circle around it.
  • No "Not done" on change requests. If it is listed, it is not done; its colour says what it is.
  • Reply, Mark done, No reply needed, Delete now show at the bottom right of a note when you point at it, in Notes and in Messages. They never cover a word: a note whose last line reaches that corner gets one short line of space under it, and the others none.
  • No Reply on a change request, in Notes and in Messages. Mark done and Delete stay. Replies already written under a change request still show.
  • No more "Sending…" or "Saving…" on the team's side: a small turning circle instead, on a note or message on its way, the urgent ping button, the review page's conversation, and Kasper's Approve button.
  • Spacing check: every kind of note looked at, light and dark (short, long, several lines, attachments, replies, team only, automatic record). One real problem found and fixed: Messages never showed a message's attachments; now they show under the words, as in Notes.
  • The whole Notes row on a card (the Notes button, the gap and the bubbles) opens Notes now. The hover on the bubbles still works.
Decided, and one choice left

Reply under a change request: off, as you decided. Your team did use it: 47 replies under change requests, 12 in the last month. Replies already written still show; a new answer is a note of its own.
