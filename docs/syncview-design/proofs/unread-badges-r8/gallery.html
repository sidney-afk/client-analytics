Unread badges, round 8

Your eight points from using it live, built and switched on only for your accounts. Every picture is the real SyncView page, from the code on GitHub, with made-up posts, people and pictures. Click any picture to see it full screen (arrow keys for the next one, Escape to close).

What changed
  • Card badges: something new is the person's picture with a small count on its top right corner, nothing else. Nothing new but still needs you: the plain coloured number beside the picture. Never both at once: when there is something new, that is what shows.
  • Card hover: only as wide as its lines need; a long line ends in "…".
  • No underline on Reply, Mark done, No reply needed or Delete, even when you point at them: they just turn darker.
  • "Waiting for your answer" is gone everywhere. A comment shows no status word; a change request still says "Change request". The To answer tab and the badge colours still carry it.
  • "Mark unread" is gone from Notes and from the Messages header.
  • Messages: Reply and No reply needed sit at the bottom left, under the message, when you point at it.
  • Threads like Reddit: a reply sits indented under the message it answers, on a thin line. At most two levels, then "replying to Casey". Notes shows the same thin line.
  • Messages header: "Open card" as a word, then a plain × at the far right. The separate Close box is gone.

The client has something new: the picture with the orange 3 on its corner, and nothing beside it.

2. Notes

No "Waiting for your answer", no "Mark unread", and the thin thread line under a note with replies.

The buttons turn darker when you point at them, with no underline.

4. Messages

Replies indented under what they answer; the pointer is on a message, so Reply and No reply needed show at its bottom left; the header ends with Open card and ×.
